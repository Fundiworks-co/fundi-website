How we work

Diagnose first. Build one tool. Stay to keep it working.

Most small businesses don't need more software. They need the one tool that fits how they already work, set up properly and supported after launch.

STEP 1

Diagnose

A half-day workshop with the owner and the team. We map where time and money go missing and put a shilling figure on it: staff hours, leakage, lost sales.

You get a problem map and a recommended fix.

STEP 2

Build

We build one tool for the biggest problem, roll it out and train your staff. A fixed price for an agreed scope, never billed by the hour.

You get the tool, trained staff and a before-and-after measure.

STEP 3

Support

A monthly retainer covers hosting, fixes and small changes, with a named person to call. The code, the database and your data are yours.

You get a tool that keeps working after we leave.

For incubators, investors and programmes

Practical support across a whole cohort, with results you can report.

Your companies get hands-on help with operations, not another training deck. You get consistent before-and-after numbers across the portfolio.

Tools we build

Starting points we adapt to each business.

Every tool starts from our own tested building blocks, so you pay for the parts that are specific to you, not for reinventing logins and settings.

Shops and small chains

Point of sale and stock

Sell on a phone or tablet, take M-Pesa and cash, close the till against what was counted, and know your real stock.

Anyone taking M-Pesa

Payments reconciliation

Match M-Pesa and bank statements to orders and invoices, and see what's missing before month end.

Walk-in and repair businesses

Queues and service

Check-in, a live queue for staff, a TV for customers waiting, and repair jobs tracked from drop-off to collection.

Sales-led teams

Sales pipelines

Lead lists, daily call queues and follow-ups, so no lead goes cold because someone forgot.

Founders, boards, investors

KPI dashboards

The numbers you rebuild every Monday, defined once and up to date, with a view your investors can open.

Growing teams

People and performance

Targets, variable pay, shift rotas, staff records and training, in one place instead of five spreadsheets.
